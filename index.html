Research academy

The SCOPE method

S

Source

Who made the claim? Is it a community report, vendor, researcher or automated feed?

C

Current?

When was it observed? Infrastructure changes owners and behaviour over time.

O

Overlap

Do independent sources agree, or are they repeating the same original report?

P

Present locally?

Does your internal telemetry show the indicator at the relevant time and asset?

E

Evidence & uncertainty

What can you conclude, what remains unknown, and what should be validated next?
